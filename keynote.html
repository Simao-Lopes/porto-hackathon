02 // Where we’re going

The vision

A 24/7 AI weight management team in your pocket, backed by human nutritionists, general practitioners, fitness coaches, and psychologists on demand.

Your AI assistant gives personalized recommendations as you log meals and activity. Human experts periodically review your progress and adjust your meal and fitness plans. They’re available for questions that need human judgment, with clinical appointments a click away and supplements or medication prescriptions offered at the professionals’ discretion.

The app guides you through your day-to-day weight management journey. The experts support you through the toughest moments.
